Starting a New Chapter

September 2026 · Andres Gamez

I'm excited to share that I've accepted an offer to join Oncor Electric Delivery as a GIS Analyst, starting September 2026 in Fort Worth, TX. This is a big step for me, and I couldn't be more thrilled about what's ahead.

In this role I'll be putting my GIS and cartography skills to work every day, and I'm especially looking forward to growing alongside a new team and taking on fresh challenges. It feels like the natural next chapter after everything I've learned so far.

A heartfelt thank-you to everyone who supported me along the way — mentors, professors, friends, and family. Your encouragement made this possible, and I'm grateful for it.

If you'd like to connect, talk maps, or just say hello, I'd love to hear from you. Reach out and let's connect.
